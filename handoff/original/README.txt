Pip | Blender handoff
Open Pip.blend in Blender 4.3.2 or newer and press Space to play the 84-frame die toss at 24 fps. Select the Pip pose rig and use Pose Mode to edit Chest, Head, Tail, both ears and both paws. The gold die has its own animated control.
Pip-v1-reference.png is the character design reference. The Blender scene is an editable, simplified reconstruction with smooth surfaces. Pip-preview.png shows the actual render.
